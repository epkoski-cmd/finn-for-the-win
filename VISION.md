# Vision

> **Finns for the Win: every Finn playing abroad, in the sports Finns care about,
> and where to watch them legally in Finland. Correct, fast on a phone, and in Finnish.**

Today the site covers football ("Finn for the Win"). The direction is to grow into
"Finns for the Win", one sport at a time, but only after football has shown that
people actually use it.

Every new idea is measured against this page. If an idea doesn't serve it, it waits.

## The moment we design for

On a match day, a Finnish fan opens the site on their phone and **within a few seconds**
knows which Finns play today, when, and where to watch legally. They trust the answer.
Once a week they glance back at how "our guys and girls" did.

## What success looks like

| Area | Success |
|---|---|
| **User experience** | The answer to "who plays today and where can I watch?" is visible without scrolling or tapping, on a phone, in Finnish. A first-time visitor understands the page without explanation. |
| **Content** | It's **correct**: no wrong clubs, no missing Finns in competitions we say we follow, streaming info that isn't out of date. One wrong answer costs more trust than ten new features earn. |
| **Features** | Few, and each one is used. A feature nobody uses is a maintenance cost, not an asset. |
| **Usage** | People come back on their own, every week, without being reminded. |
| **Upkeep** | The site runs by itself between work sessions. Hand-kept data (streaming table, exception lists) stays small enough to check a few times a season. |

## Principles that don't change as we grow

- Legal Finnish streaming only. Never betting sites, and no betting ads.
- No cookies, accounts or personal data. Settings stay in the visitor's own browser.
- Text only: no player photos, logos or club badges.
- Honest about what we don't know: unconfirmed info is marked as such.
- A new sport or competition is added only if it can be watched legally in Finland.

## Stages

1. **Friends and family** (football). Is it useful to people who know us?
2. **Strangers.** Reliable, properly licensed data, and probably ice hockey as the second sport,
   the one with the most Finns abroad and the biggest Finnish audience.
3. **Beyond.** Only if stage 2 shows real, regular use. Any way of covering costs must keep the
   principles above.

Each stage starts only when the one before it has clearly worked.
